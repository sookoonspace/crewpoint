Every main screen in Slate and sage

You chose Slate and sage with Lucide icons in Round 1. Here are the nine most-used phone screens and two laptop screens in that look, in light and dark. Once you approve them, they are the agreed look until each screen is built.

Screen

    What stays the same on every screen

    • One sage accent for actions and selection. Green, amber and red mean only done or paid, due, and overdue, each with an icon.
    • Answers are tappable chips, not dropdowns. Required questions carry a red star, and Save names what is missing.
    • Words such as "Family" come from the event's template, so a wedding or a tournament shows its own words.
    • The lakeside picture stands in for an organizer's own cover photo. Without a photo, the banner shows the event's icon.

    Your approval
